**Gianni Infantino's Grand Tour**
Gianni Infantino has turned WC26 into a continent-wide air bridge. 
Travelling aboard a Gulfstream G650 provided by Qatar Airways under the tournament's sponsorship arrangements the FIFA president has logged 30,261 miles · 48,700 km across 17 days, 27 stops, and 25 matches through June 27. (Part I)

That is roughly 1.22 times around the Earth. It also means 1.47 matches per day, 

In the last two weeks he's kept up the pace, racking up another 18,745 miles to push his tournament-long total to 49,006 miles - 78,874km.

That is close to double the Earth's circumference on this World Cup alone.
